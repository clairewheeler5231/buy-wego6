The formula

WEGO6 declares 387 mg of active blend per capsule and describes what that blend is made of: plant-based ingredients, amino acids and zinc, with apple cider vinegar and zinc named specifically. A stated strength and a published ingredient list is exactly what we want to see, and it is not something every metabolism capsule offers.

The composition is coherent rather than scattergun. A blend you can describe in a single sentence beats a wall of unfamiliar names every time.

What we look for in a blend of this size is whether the parts have a reason to sit together, and here they clearly do. Each one occupies a different corner of the same idea, and none of them is present simply to make the list longer. That restraint is worth crediting, because the easiest way to make a supplement look impressive is to add names to it, and WEGO6 has resisted the temptation.

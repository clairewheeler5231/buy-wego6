A single daily capsule, by design

The whole of WEGO6 is one capsule a day. There is no loading week, no morning-and-evening split and no escalating amount, so the routine you start on day one is identical to the routine on day sixty.

That consistency is part of why it is easy to live with. Your body meets the same 387 mg at roughly the same time every day, which is about as predictable as a supplement routine can be.

The ingredients are ones people already know

The blend is built on apple cider vinegar powder, guarana extract, dandelion root, nettle leaf, cayenne pepper, sea buckthorn extract and zinc. There is nothing exotic on that list, and most of it is already familiar from kitchens, herbal teas and everyday nutrition.

Zinc is the recognised nutrient in the formula and contributes to normal macronutrient metabolism. The rest are plants with long histories of everyday use, which is precisely the sort of company you want in a supplement you plan to take every morning.

Taking WEGO6 comfortably

Take the capsule with a full glass of water and with food rather than on a completely empty stomach. Breakfast is the natural home for it, and the food does most of the work in keeping it comfortable.

Because guarana is a natural source of caffeine, the morning also makes better sense than the evening. If you are someone who notices caffeine late in the day, that one small timing choice is worth making from the start.

Stick to the stated dose

One capsule a day is the dose, and there is no benefit in taking more. The formula is built around that single 387 mg serving, and a 30-capsule pack is designed to last exactly one month at that rate.

If you miss a day, simply carry on the next morning as normal. There is nothing to make up and nothing to double, which is one of the quieter advantages of a once-daily product.

One sensible note

If you are pregnant, breastfeeding, taking any medication or living with a medical condition, have a quick word with your doctor before starting WEGO6, as you would with any supplement.

Otherwise, keep the pack somewhere dry and out of direct sunlight, keep it away from children, and take your capsule with breakfast. That is genuinely the whole of it.
